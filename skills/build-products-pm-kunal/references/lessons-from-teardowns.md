# Lessons from teardowns (product)

Rules learnt by taking apart real products with product-teardowns, in its product mode. Each rule is a product decision that worked for a shipped product, or one worth avoiding, with the conditions under which it holds. Apply them when framing options (Step 4) and writing the decision record (Step 5). The products each rule came from are in brackets.

## Monetisation and pricing

## Metrics and experiments
- Send app events through one data layer to one analytics tool from day one, so what you measure is consistent and new tools can be added without code changes. (from srimandir)

## Trade-offs
- Start with one analytics tool. Every extra tracking vendor slows the page, adds privacy risk and splits the truth about your metrics. (from srimandir)
